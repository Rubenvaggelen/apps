## Full description

RUTU BBQ brings people together over good food. Browse the menu, choose your favorite dishes and follow your order in the RUTU BBQ app.

What can you do with RUTU BBQ?

- Explore BBQ dishes, extras, photos and prices.
- Add your favorites to your basket.
- Select available pickup or delivery options.
- Follow your order status.
- Receive order updates when you enable notifications.

Discover BBQ Regular, BBQ Extra, BBQ Family, satay and more. The available ordering and payment options are shown when you place your order. Menu items, prices, business hours and availability may vary.
