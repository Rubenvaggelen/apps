# The One – Fitness gym availability update

- Added separate gym-access options under the full gym setting:
  - Weekdays (Monday through Friday)
  - Weekend (Saturday and Sunday)
- At least one availability window must be selected when full gym access is enabled.
- Existing profiles migrate safely: previously enabled gym access initially remains available all week until edited.
- Weekly training plans now adapt to gym access. Strength sessions are moved toward gym-access days where practical; sessions outside gym access automatically use the home version.
- Cardio automatically uses the home treadmill when available, otherwise an accessible gym cardio machine or outside walking/cycling.
- Schedule and profile summaries show when the gym is available.
- Progress tracking now counts completed sessions in the current week independently of adaptive day placement.
